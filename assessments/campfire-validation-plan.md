# Campfire — Manual Validation Cycle (approved 2026-07-07)

Goal: prove someone will PAY for a screened-cohort Bitcoin focus group with
an AI-synthesized report, before any code exists. One cycle, ~2 weeks.

## The offer (draft — pitch to 3 prospects, need 1 yes)

> We recruit 5 screened Bitcoin/Nostr users matching your target profile,
> run a structured 45-min async focus group on your product question,
> pay participants in sats, and deliver a synthesized insight report in
> 72h. Flat $200. If the report is not useful, do not pay.

Prospect profiles (teams with a live product + open UX questions):
1. A wallet team shipping a new flow (e.g. a Bitcoin wallet adding stablecoins)
2. A Nostr client with retention questions
3. A freedom-tech tool with onboarding drop-off

## Participants
- 5 people, paid 10k sats each (~$10) on completion — 50k sats total (~$50)
- Recruit: Island Bitcoin community, Stacker News post, Nostr note
- Screen: 3-question form (usage frequency, tools used, willingness to talk)

## Session mechanics (no product needed)
- Async in a private Signal/WhatsApp/Discord thread over 48h
- Moderator (AI-assisted, human-supervised): 6 structured prompts,
  2 follow-ups per participant
- Synthesis: LLM pass over transcript -> themes, verbatims, recommendations;
  human edit; 3-page report

## Success gates (build/kill after ONE cycle)
- BUILD signal: customer pays full $200 without discount pressure AND asks
  when they can run another cycle
- KILL signal: cannot get 1 paying yes from 3 pitches in 2 weeks, or
  report judged "interesting but not actionable"

## Costs
~$50 sats payouts + operator time. No code.
